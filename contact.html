Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can we start after scoping?expand_more

For software and mobile apps, sprint discovery kicks off within 3 to 5 business days after contract sign-off. Urgent infrastructure deployments can be scheduled in as little as 48 hours.

Do you handle on-site networking outside Kampala?expand_more

Yes. Our field engineering units travel throughout Central, Western, Eastern and Northern Uganda, as well as cross-border sites in Rwanda, Kenya and South Sudan for enterprise structured cabling and server rack setups.

Can we drop by the Kyaliwajjala studio without an appointment?expand_more

You are welcome to visit our lounge any time between 8:00 AM and 6:30 PM on weekdays. For in-depth architecture discussions, booking in advance ensures the Lead Software Architect is free and prepared with relevant case studies.

Do you sign NDAs before preliminary discussions?expand_more

Strictly and always. We execute standard bilateral NDAs before reviewing proprietary codebases, database schematics or unannounced product mockups.
